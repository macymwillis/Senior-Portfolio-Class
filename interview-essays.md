## 1. Discuss one challenge you overcame during your academic development. What was the challenge, was it technical, nontechnical, or both, and how did you overcome it?
One challenge I overcame during my academic development was learning programming. When I first started taking any programming classes, I found them to be a real struggle. I still sometimes struggle with programming, but I have learned how to work through those challenges instead of becoming frustrated and giving up. At first, I did not reach out for help because I thought I should be able to figure everything out on my own. There were many times I thought I had the correct code written down, but there was something still that was not working correctly. Over time, I learned that programming can be very specific and detailed, and even the smallest change can affect an entire program. The smallest mistake in the code, like one character or punctuation or anything like that was different could cause something else to stop working so it ruined the entire code. Learning this helped me understand that debugging is a very important part of coding. I also became more aware of asking for help when I just don't understand something. With this being said, although programming can still be difficult and challenging for me and I am sure others, I have tried to become more patient and more persistent with rechecking myself.
